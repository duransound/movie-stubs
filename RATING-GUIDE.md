# Rating guide

A shared rubric so a 4-star from Mollie means roughly the same thing as a
4-star from you. Ratings are on a 0.5–5 scale in half-star steps — use the
whole number as the anchor and the half-star for "a little better/worse
than that."

- **5 — Instant favorite.** Would drop everything to rewatch. Talked about
  it for days after.
- **4 — Really good.** Glad we watched it, would recommend without
  hesitating. Minor flaws, if any.
- **3 — Solid / enjoyable.** A perfectly good night in. Wouldn't go out of
  the way to rewatch, but no regrets.
- **2 — Mediocre.** Something didn't land — pacing, plot, performances.
  Fine as background noise, not much more.
- **1 — Didn't work.** Actively disliked it, or it was a slog to finish.
- **0.5 — Turned it off / couldn't finish.** Reserve for the rare movie
  that just isn't worth completing.

Half-steps (e.g. 3.5) are for "better than the number below, not quite the
number above" — no need to overthink it. The point is consistency between
the two of you, not precision.
